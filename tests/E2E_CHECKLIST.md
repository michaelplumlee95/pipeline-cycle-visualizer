# Browser end-to-end checklist

Automated tests (`node tests/run.js`) cover assembling, pipeline timing, state, and generated markup against a stub DOM.
They can't see real layout, focus, or CSS, so walk through these in a browser after UI changes
(open `index.html` directly). Console must show **zero errors** throughout.

1. **Default**: loads on "Back-to-back RAW" with the picker showing that name; the table has 3 rows, the dependent `sub` and `and` each show two dashed red `ID o` cells (plus dashed `IF` cells for the `and` held behind), the summary reads Cycles 11 / Instructions 3 / Data stalls 4, and 2 explanations appear below.
2. **Examples**: step through every entry in the picker. The textarea fills, the table redraws, and "No hazards" shows no dashed cells and the text "No stalls". "Branch" shows 4 bubble cells before the instruction after `beq` (2 from `beq` waiting on `$t0`, then 2 fetch bubbles; the summary's Fetch bubbles reads 2), and "Jump" shows 1 after `j` and skips the instruction after it.
3. **Stage colors**: IF, ID, EX, MEM, WB cells match their legend swatches; stall cells are dashed red with the bubble glyph and are distinguishable from stages with color ignored (try a grayscale screenshot).
4. **Live editing**: type in the textarea. The table updates on every keystroke, focus and caret never jump, and the picker switches to "Custom". Picking "Custom" afterwards leaves the text alone.
5. **Errors**: enter `bogus $t0`. A red `Line 1: ...` message appears under the textarea, the table is replaced by "Fix the errors", and the summary and explanations clear (no empty bar left where the summary was). Fixing the line restores everything.
6. **Edge programs**: an empty textarea shows "Enter a program"; `top: j top` stops at 200 instructions with the red notice in the summary and does not hang the page; a long program (30+ instructions) scrolls the table horizontally inside its card with the instruction column pinned on the left and no page-level horizontal scroll. While scrolled right, nothing shows through or beside the pinned column, and the scrollbar is dark, not white.
7. **Linked highlights**: hover a stall explanation; its dashed cells get a purple outline. Hover a dashed cell; its explanation highlights. Tab into a table row and an explanation (both focusable); the focus ring is visible.
8. **Card contrast**: cards are clearly distinct from the page background, and buttons and the dropdown still read as controls.
9. **Narrow viewport** (~375px; if the browser window can't be resized, load `index.html` in a 375px-wide iframe): there is no horizontal page scroll, the layout collapses to one column (Program above Pipeline), the picker and textarea fit without overflow, and the table scrolls inside its card instead of breaking the page.
10. **Pages build**: after pushing, the published site matches the local page and the console is clean (Inter font loads from Google Fonts).
11. **Datapath default**: on load the Datapath card shows "Cycle 5 of 11" for "Back-to-back RAW". The IF, ID, and MEM/WB parts are lit in their stage colors, EX shows a dashed red region labeled `bubble`, and the caption below lists each stage with a reason for the bubble. Column 5 of the table is marked (blue header, slightly lighter cells).
12. **Cycle selection**: click a cycle header, then a cell in another column; the datapath follows and the marked column moves. Prev/Next step, and disable at cycles 1 and N. With focus on the diagram, the left and right arrow keys step. Tab to a cycle header and press Enter; it selects. Table scroll position does not jump when selecting.
13. **Opcode paths**: for each of `add`, `addi`, `lw`, `sw`, `beq`, `j`, `nop` (alone in the editor), step through all cycles and compare the lit parts with the "Opcode to lit parts" list in `design.md`. In particular `sw` never lights the write-back path, `beq` lights the branch adder in EX and the AND/PCSrc path in MEM, and `j` lights only the jump wire in ID.
14. **Datapath linking**: hover a stage label above the diagram (and a caption line); the matching table row gets a lighter background. Hover a stall cell; the same row's label lights.
15. **Datapath narrow viewport** (~375px): the diagram scrolls horizontally inside its own box at legible size, the page itself has no horizontal scroll, and the Prev/Next buttons and caption fit.
16. **Datapath errors**: `bogus $t0` replaces the status with "Fix the errors in the program to see the datapath." and clears the caption; an empty editor says "Enter a program". Restoring valid text brings the diagram back without a console error.
17. **Forwarding toggle**: the Pipeline card shows "No forwarding" (highlighted) and "Forwarding". Click Forwarding on "Back-to-back RAW": the dashed stall cells disappear, the summary reads Cycles 7 / Data stalls 0 / Forwards 3, the EX cells of `sub` and `and` show a small `fwd` marker, and two teal-edged explanation lines appear (hover one: its EX cell is outlined). Click No forwarding: everything returns to 11 cycles and 4 stalls. The selected datapath cycle does not change.
18. **Load-use with forwarding**: on "Load then use" with forwarding on, `add` still shows exactly one dashed `ID` cell; the explanation says even with forwarding a load's data is not ready until the end of MEM.
19. **Datapath forwarding hardware**: with forwarding off there are no forwarding muxes or unit in the diagram, and the straight data wires reach the ALU. With it on, two small muxes sit in front of the ALU, the Forwarding unit appears below it, and the straight wires are gone. On "Back-to-back RAW" cycle 4, `sub` in EX lights ForwardA, the EX/MEM feedback bus, and the unit; the ID/EX input of that mux stays gray. Cycle 5 (`and`) lights both buses. Nothing overlaps badly in a zoomed screenshot, and the diagram still scrolls inside its card at ~375px.
